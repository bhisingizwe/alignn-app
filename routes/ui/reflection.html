💭 Guided Reflection Prompts
  • What am I feeling right now?
  • What may be contributing to this feeling?
  • What do I need today?
  • What is one small next step I can take?
❤️ Emotional Processing Support
  • Name the emotion without judging it.
  • Notice where you feel it in your body.
  • Remind yourself that emotions are temporary.
  • Ask what this emotion may be trying to tell you.
🌱 Grounding Questions
  • What are three things I can see right now?
  • What is one thing I can hear?
  • What is one thing I can touch?
  • What is within my control today?
